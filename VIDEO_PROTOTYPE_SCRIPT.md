## 🎯 Pro Tips for Recording Your Video

1. **Resolution & Audio:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) with a clear microphone. Ensure background noise is muted.
2. **Smooth Cursor Movements:** Avoid fast, jerky mouse movements. Point deliberately at what you are speaking about.
3. **Pacing:** Speak at a steady, confident pace (~130–140 words per minute).
4. **Pre-load Tabs:** Have `http://localhost:3000`, `http://localhost:3000/test-lab`, `http://localhost:3000/security`, and `http://localhost:3000/threats` ready in browser tabs so navigation is seamless.
